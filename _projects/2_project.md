During surgery, accurately assessing a patient's level of nociception (pain processing) is critical for appropriate anesthetic dosing — yet it remains difficult to measure directly. This project develops principled statistical models that fuse multiple physiological signals to infer nociceptive state in real time.

**Approach:** We combine signals such as heart rate variability, electrodermal activity, and other autonomic markers into a unified multisensor framework. By modeling the joint dynamics of these signals, we can produce continuous, real-time estimates of nociceptive state that are more robust than any single-channel approach.

**Impact:** Better nociception monitoring can reduce over- or under-dosing of analgesics, improving patient outcomes and recovery.

*Published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (2024).*
