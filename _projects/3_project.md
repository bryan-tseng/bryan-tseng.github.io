Treatment-resistant depression (TRD) affects millions of patients who do not respond to standard antidepressants. Anesthetic agents such as ketamine have shown remarkable rapid-acting antidepressant effects, but the underlying mechanisms remain poorly understood.

**Approach:** We synthesize clinical insights with mechanistic neuroscience to map how anesthetic drugs modulate brain circuits implicated in depression. This work spans EEG-based brain state characterization, pharmacological modeling, and systematic review of clinical evidence.

**Patent:** This research led to a provisional patent filed with the MIT Technology Licensing Office for combinations of anesthetics as therapies for TRD (MIT 25342).

*Published in the Annual Review of Neuroscience (2025). Work conducted at the Neural Statistics Research Laboratory, MIT Picower Institute.*
