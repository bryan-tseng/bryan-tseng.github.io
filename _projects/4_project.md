Electroencephalography (EEG) provides a direct window into brain activity during anesthesia and critical care, yet it remains underutilized clinically. This project focuses on translating EEG into actionable tools for anesthesiologists and intensivists.

**Approach:** We characterize the physiological signatures of different anesthetic brain states using spectral and statistical analysis of EEG signals. These signatures can guide real-time dosing decisions and active management of unconsciousness depth.

**Scope:** The work covers both the operating room (surgical anesthesia) and the ICU (sedation management), providing a unified framework for interpreting EEG across clinical contexts.

*Published in Anesthesiology (2025). Work conducted at the Neural Statistics Research Laboratory, MIT Picower Institute.*
